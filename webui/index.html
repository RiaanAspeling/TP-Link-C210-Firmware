Choose a rootfs.squashfs (recommended) or a full .bin.

The image is checksummed in your browser and re-checked on the camera before anything is written. A rootfs update keeps your wifi and password; the camera reboots on its own. Do not cut power during the update.

SD card & recording

Checking card…

A motion clip length is the pre-motion buffer + the detected movement + the keep recording after motion tail. The detector often keeps seeing motion for a few seconds after you stop (auto-exposure settling), so the clip runs a little past your movement. Max clip length doesn't shorten a clip — it splits a longer event into files of that length, which can leave a short trailing file; raise it to keep an event in one file. Raise sensitivity if motion is missed.

Recording muxes the already-encoded stream — no re-encode; the source is what's saved (the sub-stream is far lighter). Formatting erases everything on the card.
