Firmware update

Choose a rootfs.squashfs (recommended) or a full .bin.

The image is checksummed in your browser and re-checked on the camera before anything is written. A rootfs update keeps your wifi and password; the camera reboots on its own. Do not cut power during the update.

Device name

The camera's hostname, the centre video overlay, and the name ONVIF / network discovery shows. Letters, digits and hyphens only (no spaces).

Web login password

Username root

6–64 characters. This is the password for this web interface and SSH. Leave blank to keep it unchanged.

ONVIF / RTSP credentials

Used by ONVIF / RTSP clients (NVRs, viewers). Separate from the web login. 1–32 characters, no spaces.

Recording

Recording muxes the already-encoded stream — no re-encode; the source is what's saved (the sub-stream is far lighter). Motion trigger settings are on the Motion tab; browse and format the card from the 🎞 Recordings button on the main screen.

Motion detection

Motion zones

Loading preview…

Click a cell to ignore motion there (red = ignored). Green flashes show where motion is being detected right now. Changes save immediately.

A motion clip length is the pre-motion buffer + the detected movement + the keep recording after motion tail. The detector often keeps seeing motion for a few seconds after you stop (auto-exposure settling), so the clip runs a little past your movement. Raise sensitivity if motion is missed. These settings only affect motion-triggered recording (Recording tab, trigger Motion or both).
